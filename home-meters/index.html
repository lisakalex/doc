Gas and water are in cubic metres (m³), electricity in kWh. Gas and electricity meters were replaced at the start of 2018, and the water meter in December 2025; the dashed line marks the change. The old gas meter counted in hundreds of cubic feet, so its figures have been converted to m³ (× 2.832) to compare with the new meter; its raw readings are kept as they were. When a reading was skipped, the use between the two readings either side is shared equally across the months in between. Data: usage.csv.
