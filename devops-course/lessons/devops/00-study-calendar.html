Christmas lands in phase 3

Weeks 11 and 12 cover 21 December to 3 January, with three bank holidays. If those weeks run short, the plan's own rule applies: let it slip by a week or two and keep the depth. The 24 weeks can stretch to 28. What should never shrink is Kubernetes in phase 5.

  1. 326 Oct – 1 Nov9 hThis week

  1. 723 Nov – 29 Nov9 hThis week
